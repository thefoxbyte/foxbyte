# CLA signatures

This branch exists only to hold `signatures/cla.json`, the record of who has
signed [CLA.md](https://github.com/thefoxbyte/foxbyte/blob/main/CLA.md). It is
written by `.github/workflows/cla.yml` and is not part of the product — it shares
no history with `main` on purpose.

Signatures are kept here rather than on a hosted service because they are the
evidence that a licence was granted, and sections 19(1) and 30 of the Copyright
Act, 1957 want a licence in writing and signed. Keeping them in this repository
means the commit, its author and its timestamp are ours and outlive any third
party's terms of service.

**Do not protect this branch.** The CLA action commits to it directly, and a
branch rule here would stop a signature from ever being recorded.
